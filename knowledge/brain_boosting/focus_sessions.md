# Focus Session Design

Tags: focus, session, timer, pomodoro, deep work, study, energy

Focus sessions work best when the timer length matches the learner's energy level. If energy is low, use a short session of 20 to 30 minutes with one clear task. If energy is stable, use 45 to 60 minutes for deeper study. Avoid starting with a long session after a distracted day because it makes failure more likely.

Each session should have one visible target, such as solving five problems, reviewing one lecture section, or writing one page of notes. A clear target reduces task switching and gives the student a concrete finish line.

Breaks should be real recovery, not another high-stimulation loop. Good breaks include stretching, drinking water, walking, breathing slowly, or preparing the next study material. Short-form video breaks often make it harder to return to the next session.

For brain boosting, use the "start small, then extend" technique: begin with 10 minutes if resistance is high, then continue into a normal focus block once momentum appears.
